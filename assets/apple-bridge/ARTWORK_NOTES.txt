APPLE BRIDGE / CLASSIC iPHONE VISUALS

The Apple Bridge brand icon is the existing res/drawable/apple_bridge_icon.png
from Wakkatobakka's public Apple-Bridge-0.0.8.apk. It is used intact, not
redesigned or replaced. The hero and social image use this same icon.

listed in the maintained game guide, presented with rounded framing and
a classic system-style glass highlight. No generated game icons or
