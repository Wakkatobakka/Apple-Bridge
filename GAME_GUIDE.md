# Apple Bridge — Game Guide

> **Old iPhone games. Back in your hands.**

Ten games from the early iPhone era, brought together on Android with their original gameplay, touchscreen controls, and tilt inputs where the game uses them.

Pick a game, download its matching original IPA, and load it in Apple Bridge. The player supplies the compatibility bridges; you supply the original game files.

**[Get started](#get-started) · [Choose a game](#choose-a-game) · [Help](#help-and-troubleshooting) · [Credits and sources](#credits-and-sources)**

## Get started

1. **Install Apple Bridge.** Use an Android 11+ device with ARM64 support, and an AB build that includes the game you want to play.
2. **Download the matching original.** Choose **Download original IPA** in the game's entry below. Keep the `.ipa` intact.
3. **Open AB and tap Load Game.** Select the downloaded IPA and let the import finish.
4. **Tap the game's tile to play.** Import the original once; launch it from your collection afterward.

No extraction or manual patching is needed. The original iOS version in a filename identifies the old game edition; it is not an Android requirement.

**About testing:** Each game is personally playtested through its tutorial and into the first level, often further. Full start-to-finish completion has not been verified for every title, so later bugs or progression issues may still exist. Your installed AB release determines which games and bridge revisions are available.

## Castlevania Puzzle: Encore of the Night

**Puzzle RPG · Touch · Landscape**

Explore Dracula's castle through falling-block battles. This is Castlevania with a very different rhythm: building chains, clearing blocks, and working through puzzle encounters.

**[Download original IPA](https://archive.org/download/Castlevania_Puzzle__Encore_of_the_Night_1.0.10_ios_3.0/Castlevania_Puzzle__Encore_of_the_Night_1.0.10_ios_3.0.ipa) · [View source page](https://archive.org/details/Castlevania_Puzzle__Encore_of_the_Night_1.0.10_ios_3.0)**

**Supported original:** v1.0.10 · 23.37 MB  
**Filename:** `Castlevania_Puzzle__Encore_of_the_Night_1.0.10_ios_3.0.ipa`

**Playing:** Drag to move falling blocks, then tap and release to rotate them. The original Options menu includes Puzzle Touch Sensitivity and Swipe to drop blocks. Follow the in-game tutorials for exploration, items, spells, and combat.

**Good to know:** Spend a moment with the original sensitivity settings if block placement feels awkward; they are part of the game itself.

## Help and troubleshooting

### AB says the original does not match

Check the exact edition and filename in the game's entry. Another version or repack may contain different files even when the title looks identical. The bridge verifies the original's contents during import; renaming an unsupported file does not make it compatible.

Use the linked IPA as an intact file. If your browser adds a suffix such as `(1)`, the unchanged contents still identify the supported original. Optional file fingerprints are listed below.

### Tilt feels wrong

Hold the phone in the position you actually want to play in. Mass Effect Galaxy is sensitive to the orientation at launch. Doom Resurrection calibrates at the start of each level and has **Pause → Calibrate**. Silent Hill: The Escape has its original heart alignment control. Use the advice for your specific game.

### I want to pause or leave a game

Use the original in-game pause control to pause. **Android Back exits gameplay.** Save through the game's own save or checkpoint system before leaving when applicable.

### Something breaks

Use **SAVE REPORT** in the game and keep the generated `.txt` from Downloads. For library or import problems, AB also has **Save report** in its overflow menu.

When reporting a problem, include:

- Game title and original edition.
- Apple Bridge version and Android device.
- What happened and how to reproduce it.
- The saved report, plus a short recording or screenshot when useful.

### Updating Apple Bridge

Install updates over the same AB edition to retain its collection and saves. Keep the existing app data. Original save and checkpoint behavior varies by game.
